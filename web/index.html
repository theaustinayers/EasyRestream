RTMP Relay

One source. Three independent outputs. No transcoding or placeholder.

Every destination receives your OBS resolution, bitrate and codecs unchanged. Verify platform compatibility before going live. Stop disconnects the destination; reconnecting may not preserve the broadcast.

Forwarding means FFmpeg reports output progress, not verified platform broadcast health. Source failures trigger retries (up to 30 seconds between attempts). All outputs start stopped after a service restart.
